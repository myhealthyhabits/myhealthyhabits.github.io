Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Summary

We collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and you can delete it any time.

Guest mode

Without an account, everything you log (foods, workouts, steps, goals) stays only on your device in your browser’s storage. We don’t receive it.

With an account

If you create an account, your email address and your logs (foods, workouts, steps, goals, settings) are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address. We do not access your contacts, Gmail, Drive, or anything else.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and Nutrition Facts labels. Scanning happens on your device; images are not uploaded or stored by us. Barcode numbers may be looked up in public food databases.

We don’t sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the App (such as Supabase and, for sign-in emails, our email provider) or when required by law.

Payments (future)

If paid features are added, payments will be processed by Stripe or Google Play. The App does not store your card number.

Delete your account or data

Guest data: clear the App’s site data in your browser. Account data: email myhealthyhabitsapp0@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your account and synced data.

Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
