## Accounts / sync (when ready)
- Recommended: Supabase. Free plan covers 50k monthly users, a 500 MB database and 1 GB storage, but projects pause after a week of no activity. Pro is $25/mo.
- Alternative: Firebase. The free Spark plan includes 1 GiB, 50k reads and 20k writes a day. Blaze is pay-as-you-go.
- All saving already goes through one storage layer, and each save is timestamped for sync.

## Subscription
- Stripe charges 2.9% + 30c per payment, so a $1/mo price loses about a third to fees. Use $1.99/mo or a yearly price.
- Google Play: $25 developer fee, 12 testers for 14 days, and Play Billing takes 15%.
